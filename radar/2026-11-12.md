## arXiv — fresh and relevant

- [DailyWildfireCAN: A Dataset and Benchmark for Next-Day Wildfire Spread Prediction in Canada](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14207v1) — Wildfires represent a critical ecological and socio-economic challenge in Canada, where vast boreal forests and a warming climate have amplified the frequency and severity of extreme fire seasons.
- [RS-JEPA: Remote Sensing Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture with Scan-Path Views for Cross-Sensor Image Retrieval](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14496v1) — Cross-sensor remote sensing image retrieval aims to find scenes with the same content across heterogeneous sensors, such as SAR and optical or panchromatic (PAN) and multispectral (MS) imagery, without manual labels.
- [ViGA-FLAME: Aligning Latent Visual and Geometric Representations for Robust 3D Head Expression Estimation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14218v1) — Estimating 3D head expression from a single image requires mapping visual appearance to underlying facial geometry.
- [Sim(3)-TurboReg: Robust and Efficient Scale-Aware Registration via Tri-Scale Consistency](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14135v1) — Feed-forward 3D foundation models recover geometry at high fidelity, yet inference memory constraints keep them from processing long sequences natively.
- [TaoMate-R1: Temporal Role Specialization for Streaming Multi-Reference Video Generation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14307v1) — Streaming multi-reference video generation establishes a scene from specified people and objects, then continues it under changing instructions.
- [CroCoMAE: Cross-modal self-supervised collaborative representation learning for SAR-optical remote sensing data](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14124v1) — Remote sensing increasingly captures the Earth's surface through complementary sensors such as SAR and optical, yet ground-truth annotations remain scarce, limiting supervised approaches.
- [A Lightweight Differentiable Method for Real-Time Skin Rendering and Digitization](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14451v1) — Inverse rendering for skin typically relies on offline path tracing to estimate physical properties, which fail to map directly to the approximate shaders utilized in interactive applications, such as game engines.
- [UGG: Unified Generative Geometry Model](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14440v1) — Understanding 4D scenes is a core challenge in visual intelligence, requiring models to jointly capture scene geometry, appearance, and dynamics from partial observations.
