## Papers

- [PDRVLA: A Vision-Language-Action Model with Physical Dynamics Representations](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.37381) (▲ 134) — Vision--Language--Action (VLA) models generate robot actions from visual observations and language instructions, but their understanding of physical dynamics remains limited.
- [VibeVideoBench: Evaluating Multimodal Agents on Code-Driven Video Generation](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.35864) (▲ 46) — Diffusion video models produce striking clips, but they cannot reliably render an exact formula, a correct chart, a rule-consistent game or narration synchronized to on-screen events.
- [ThinkWorld: World Imagination Reinforces Chain-of-Dynamics Reasoning](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.37666) (▲ 44) — Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models have made substantial progress in robotic manipulation, but their generalization remains constrained by the diversity and coverage of action-labeled robot demonstrations.
- [LociArt: A Unified Paradigm for Free-Style Local Video Stylization](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.36456) (▲ 28) — Video stylization typically transforms entire scenes, while local artistic editing requires precise region--style association, spatial control, and temporal consistency.
- [On the SPOT: Enhancing Speculative Decoding for Diffusion Language Models via Commit-Order Prediction](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.22543) (▲ 21) — Diffusion language models (DLMs) promise parallel text generation, but in practice face an unresolved speed-quality trade-off.
- [Channel Selection for Mixture-of-Experts Decoding Needs No Calibration Data](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.37386) (▲ 17) — Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models are bottlenecked by loading routed-expert weights from memory during interactive decoding at small batch sizes.
- [How Far Can Coding Agents Go in Visual Generation?](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.19025) (▲ 13) — Modern image generation largely relies on specialized generative models that leave the process of image construction implicit in learned parameters.
- [What Effort Level Should I Choose? On the Economics of Reasoning Effort in LLM Agents](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.34491) (▲ 12) — Generative models increasingly expose a reasoning effort setting that lets users scale inference-time compute, trading higher cost for potentially improved performance.
- [RynnWorld-Latent: A Cross-Embodiment World Model Conditioned on Universal Latent Actions](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.36791) (▲ 8) — Learning controllable world dynamics from heterogeneous interaction videos is challenging because robot embodiments use incompatible action spaces, while human videos lack native control labels.
- [Your Policy Is Secretly a Harness: The Decision Geometry of Agent Interfaces](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.37255) (▲ 6) — Language model agents are post-trained and deployed through a harness, the prompts, tools, and control logic that present a task to the model and execute its outputs.
