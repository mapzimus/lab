## arXiv — fresh and relevant

- [DailyWildfireCAN: A Dataset and Benchmark for Next-Day Wildfire Spread Prediction in Canada](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14207v1) — Wildfires represent a critical ecological and socio-economic challenge in Canada, where vast boreal forests and a warming climate have amplified the frequency and severity of extreme fire seasons.
- [RS-JEPA: Remote Sensing Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture with Scan-Path Views for Cross-Sensor Image Retrieval](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14496v1) — Cross-sensor remote sensing image retrieval aims to find scenes with the same content across heterogeneous sensors, such as SAR and optical or panchromatic (PAN) and multispectral (MS) imagery, without manual labels.
- [ViGA-FLAME: Aligning Latent Visual and Geometric Representations for Robust 3D Head Expression Estimation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14218v1) — Estimating 3D head expression from a single image requires mapping visual appearance to underlying facial geometry.
