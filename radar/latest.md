- [U-Space: Uncovering When and Why Uncertainty Arises in Language Models](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.09087) (▲ 38) — Large language models are informing decisions with ever-higher stakes.
- [Post-Training Frontier Text-to-Image Models by Composing Preference and Rubric Rewards](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.02967) (▲ 29) — Recent text-to-image generation models have achieved remarkable visual quality, but improving them through post-training remains challenging because no single reward signal captures the full range of human preference.
- [Embodied Turing Machines: Stateful Code for Robot Recursive Self-Improvement](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.12369) (▲ 19) — Most robot policies keep a model in the control loop: a VLA maps observations to actions, and an Agent Harness, such as Agent-as-Policy or Harness VLA queries a VLM for decision making at run time.
