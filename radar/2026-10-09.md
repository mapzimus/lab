## Papers

- [WorldGuide: Goal-Directed Video World Model for Procedural Task Execution](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.12459) (▲ 4) — Video generators and video-based world models can synthesize plausible visual trajectories, but long-horizon procedural tasks require generation to adapt to what has actually been produced.
- [A GPU-Parallel Framework for Heterogeneous Multi-Task Reinforcement Learning](https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.03335) (▲ 1) — GPU-parallel simulation provides abundant robot interaction, but existing benchmarks rarely combine this scale with heterogeneous manipulation tasks and standardized multi-task RL evaluation.
- [MC-Sparse: Deconstructing and Closing the Dense-Sparse Attention Gap in Diffusion Transformers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.06801) (▲ 27) — Sparse attention is a primary approach to reducing the latency of diffusion transformers in long-sequence generation tasks, such as video and high-resolution 3D asset generation.
- [SatNav: A Scalable Benchmark for Long-Horizon UAV Vision-Language Navigation from Satellite Imagery](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.31507) (▲ 1) — Urban uncrewed aerial vehicle (UAV) vision-language navigation (VLN) requires agents to follow instructions across extended urban spaces, inherently demanding long-term memory and geospatial grounding.
- [Foundations of Large Language Models](https://huggingface.co/papers/2501.09223) (▲ 30) — This is a book about large language models.
- [Beyond Spatio-Temporal Priors: A Generalizable Approach for Dense Correspondence Matching](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.12421) (▲ 25) — Dense correspondence matching has historically been bounded by simplifying spatio-temporal priors, such as smooth motion and rigid geometry.
- [U-Space: Uncovering When and Why Uncertainty Arises in Language Models](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.09087) (▲ 25) — Large language models are informing decisions with ever-higher stakes.
- [Post-Training Frontier Text-to-Image Models by Composing Preference and Rubric Rewards](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.02967) (▲ 23) — Recent text-to-image generation models have achieved remarkable visual quality, but improving them through post-training remains challenging because no single reward signal captures the full range of human preference.
- [LEGO: A Lifting-Free Approach for Exocentric-to-Egocentric Video Generation](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.12442) (▲ 18) — Generating an egocentric video from a single exocentric recording is a challenging case of novel view synthesis, as the two cameras share little overlap and much of the target view is unobserved.
- [Embodied Turing Machines: Stateful Code for Robot Recursive Self-Improvement](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.12369) (▲ 10) — Most robot policies keep a model in the control loop: a VLA maps observations to actions, and an Agent Harness, such as Agent-as-Policy or Harness VLA queries a VLM for decision making at run time.
