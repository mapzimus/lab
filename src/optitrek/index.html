Optitrek

A road-trip optimizer. It picks the stops and the order for the fastest drive through hundreds of national parks, using real road routing (OSRM) and Google's OR-Tools solver. Its first job was beating Randal Olson's famous 2015 road trip: the result is 13.8% less driving time and 28.9% fewer miles.

Each map below is a full interactive route. Some are large (up to 16 MB), so give them a moment to load. Source: mapzimus/optitrek.
