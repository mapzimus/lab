Method & sources
Net = new-construction permits − completed demolitions in the cell. Cells with no activity that year are left dim.

BSEED publishes building permits from 2019 onward only; demolitions run back to 2014. Before 2019 the net is demolitions alone — new construction happened and is simply not in the published record.

Demolitions: City of Detroit Completed Demolitions. New builds: BSEED Building Permits, permit_type of New or Foundation Only. Boundary: Census TIGERweb. Basemap © CARTO, © OpenStreetMap contributors.
